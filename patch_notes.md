## 🏘️ Once Upon a Town v0.0.13
### Builder enhancement:
- Fixed the flickering issue with tool when there two builders were in the village
- Made the builder avoid trapdoor to improve its pathfinding and avoid being stuck
- Fixed the issue making the builder taking a random walk while moving and building
- Removed the 3 minutes wait when the builder wasn't able to build a structure that some time made him wait for nothing instead of doing something else
### Gameplay improvements: 
- Added a new path for the industrial village 'excavators' that will lead to stone and ore extraction buildings
- Added a ton of new resources unlocked for the buildings, making some path more rewarding
- The weight got nerf again, it will not be a requirement to change your era being less restrictive and allowing for more building composition and a smoother progress into eras. The weight is already a nerf on its own so I didnt wanted being another nerf to the era
- Renamed the 'era x x/x' on top of the catalog for 'space remaining : x/x', it should help players to understand weight better
- Updated the era widget to make it more clear about which buildings are going to be unlocked into the catalog following which era path you select
- Fixed a ton of bugs that made the starting of the village having some building disconnected from the road, placed at the wrong heigh or when the builder was stuck facing a street and not building. Early game should be smoother and more straight forward
- The village cannot develop on water anymore, fixing some issues with npc drowning or always slowed during underwater construction
- Improved the interface of the quest needing you to clear obstacles, so it's easier to understand which blocks you had to remove. Also improved the system to better detect obstacles
- Added the era 3 for each orientation, expending a bit more the gameplay loop and reach a total of 50 weight for the village
- Added new quests and notes
### New buildings:
- Open mine with it's 6 levels
- Settlement (industrial) received 5 upgrades
- Improved a ton of already existing NBT

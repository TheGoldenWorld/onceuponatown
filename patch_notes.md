## 🏘️ Once Upon a Town v0.0.14 | Autonomous villages !
### Autonomous villages:
- Following its orientation and era, the village will now automatically select buildings through the time when the resource conditions are met. This would help villages to feel more alive. The building added by the village will be locked, you cannot remove it
- You can still add buildings by your own if you want specific resources to be produced by the village
- The village will also change the era automatically, your only choice will the orientation work at era 2, the village will swap its development branch following your choice
- This system is 100% customizable in the era JSON, you can configure the building placement sequence to optimize the village choice and resource production
### Village life improvements:
- All the custom entities from OuaT are now affected by a day and night cycle. The schedule is customizable so you are free to make the npc work and rest at the time you want. When the night fall, the npc are going to look for a bed in a structure you can specify
- The bed is now way more important in the early game. Every house contains one or two and the town center also contains one (builder house). This changed the whole early game, a bed being a very basic furniture you will have to produce in the village (workshop + sheep field)
- Added a new npc : Lumberjack ! The lumberjack is a whole new entity that will bring more life to the village. You can add one by building the lumberjack building era 2. It will cut the tree in specific buildings and replant a sapling. This is also customizable, it can cut any tree in builds
### Gameplay improvements:
- The streets are no longer one shot, they are built block by block like any other structure
- Reworked the block placement system to delay specific blocks, like water, lily pads and entities. This is also fixing the issue when the builder could block itself while builder structure with water
### Other changes
- Added 6 levels for the Settlement pastoral
- Added the final levels for the Settlement industrial
- Added new clothes for the lumberjack
- Made some internal code changes to allow the mod being 100% datapack friendly, a custom modded culture is being worked on, more to come soon
